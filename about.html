About Us

Small Steps Big Wins In A Busy World.

Many people who struggle with executive function often find traditional to-do lists overwhelming because they don’t know where to start. This leads to procrastination, a snowball effect of unfinished tasks, and increased stress.

Addison helps you plan, focus, and follow through by giving you tools that support your executive function in real time.

Built with care.

Our team is focused on designing tools that feel calm, useful, and supportive. We care about thoughtful product design, accessibility, and creating systems that meet people where they are.
