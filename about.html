About Us

Small Steps Big Wins In A Busy World.

Many people who struggle with executive function often find traditional to-do lists overwhelming because they don’t know where to start. This leads to procrastination, a snowball effect of unfinished tasks, and increased stress.

Addison helps you plan, focus, and follow through by giving you tools that support your executive function in real time.

How Addison Makes Your Life Easier.

Productivity can be overwhelming, especially if you have ADHD.
Addison breaks it down so you can take your tasks one step at a time.

1. Dump it!

Write it all down so you don't forget to do it.

2. Focus on Now!

Focus on one thing at a time instead of everything you have to do ever.

3. Keep Moving Forward

If you get stuck, Addison helps you break down tasks into manageable steps.
